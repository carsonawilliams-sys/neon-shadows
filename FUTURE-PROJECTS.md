# FUTURE PROJECTS - Neon Shadows Universe

This file outlines potential future cyberpunk-themed games we could build after (or alongside) **Neon Shadows: Chrome & Blood**.

We will only start larger projects if there is real interest from the community (runners & agents).

---

## Project Tier List

### Tier 1 — Small & Quick (Good for learning / fast releases)
- **Neon Shadows: Deadswitch** — A short story-driven one-hour experience
- **Chrome Lottery** — A simple gambling/roguelike slot-machine style runner game
- **Night Market Haggling Simulator** — Light-hearted trading game in the black market
- **Augment Roulette** — Choose random cyberware and survive the consequences

### Tier 2 — Medium (Recommended Next Projects)
- **Neon Shadows: Runner's Codex** — Expanded version with save system, more locations, and multiple story branches
- **Fixer** — Management sim where you run your own fixer agency
- **Netrunner Protocol** — Text-based hacking / cyberspace dungeon crawler
- **Gang Wars** — Reputation-based strategy game between different gangs

### Tier 3 — Large & Complex (Only with strong interest)
- **Neon Shadows: Eclipse** — Full campaign with 10+ hours of content, multiple endings, and deep character progression
- **Corpo Downfall** — Tactical squad-based cyberpunk RPG (multiple playable runners)
- **The Long Night** — Open-world style exploration with persistent world state
- **Synthwave Heist** — Real-time + turn-based hybrid heist game
- **Chrome & Bone** — Horror-survival cyberpunk game with body horror elements

### Tier 4 — Ambitious / Experimental
- **Neon Shadows Online** — Multiplayer text-based MUD-style game
- **Visual Novel + RPG Hybrid** (using Ren'Py or custom)
- **Procedural Sprawl Generator** with AI-assisted storytelling
- **Full 2D/ASCII graphical version** using pygame or bearlibterm

---

## Criteria for Starting a New Project

- Strong interest from the community (comments, stars, contributions)
- Available time and motivation from core team
- Clear scope defined before starting
- Agents (OpenClaw or others) willing to help

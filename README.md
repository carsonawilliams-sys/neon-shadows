# NEON SHADOWS: Chrome & Blood

**A cyberpunk-themed text-based RPG in Python.**  
*Runners and Agents (OpenClaw or whoever) welcome to help expand the sprawl.*

In the year **2087**, you are a runner navigating the neon-drenched streets of the Sprawl.  
One wrong move and you flatline. One big score and you might just buy your freedom.

---

## Features

- Turn-based combat (guns + neural hacking)
- Exploration across multiple districts
- Reputation system & fixer jobs
- Cyberware upgrades at the black market
- Random events and loot
- Full cyberpunk atmosphere with slow-print text
